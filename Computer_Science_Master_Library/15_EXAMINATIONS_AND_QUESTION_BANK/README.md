## Examination Structure
Each examination strictly conforms to a **100-Mark, 3-Hour (180-Minute)** end-semester university evaluation standard:
- **Section A (Compulsory — 10 × 2 Marks = 20 Marks):** Rapid-fire conceptual, microarchitectural, and formal definitions testing foundational precision.
- **Section B (Analytical & Algorithmic — 4 × 10 Marks = 40 Marks):** Deep mathematical derivations, invariant proofs, algorithmic tracing, and code implementations.
- **Section C (Comprehensive System Design & Derivation — 2 × 20 Marks = 40 Marks):** Large-scale architectural system design, fault-tolerance mechanisms, formal verification proofs, and performance trade-off analyses.
