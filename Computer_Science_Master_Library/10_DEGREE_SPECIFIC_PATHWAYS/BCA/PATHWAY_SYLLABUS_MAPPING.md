# BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications) Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA)  
**Curricular Governance:** Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU / Pune University) & AICTE Guidelines  
**Standard Duration:** 3 Years (6 Semesters)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 1 (Absolute Beginner) through Level 5 (Professional Software Engineering)**.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Target Outcomes
The Bachelor of Computer Applications curriculum is designed for modern enterprise software development, database administration, web and mobile platforms, and industry-standard software engineering practices. The emphasis is on building resilient, scalable, production-ready applications with strong software architecture, REST/gRPC APIs, and automated CI/CD deployment pipelines.
