# BCS (Bachelor of Computer Science / B.Sc. CS) Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Computer Science (BCS / B.Sc. Computer Science)  
**Curricular Governance:** Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU CBCS Regulations) & UGC Guidelines  
**Standard Duration:** 3 Years (6 Semesters) / 4-Year Honours (8 Semesters under NEP 2020)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 1 (Absolute Beginner) through Level 4 (Advanced Undergraduate)**, with Level 5 & 6 electives.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Graduate Attributes
The Bachelor of Computer Science curriculum emphasizes rigorous mathematical foundations, theoretical computer science, systems programming in C/C++, algorithmic design, and relational database management. Graduates are expected to understand microarchitectural trade-offs, formal languages, operating system kernels, and clean software implementation.
