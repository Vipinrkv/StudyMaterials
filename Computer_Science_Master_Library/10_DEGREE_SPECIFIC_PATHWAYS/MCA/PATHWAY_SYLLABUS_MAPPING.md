# MCA (Master of Computer Applications) Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Master of Computer Applications (MCA)  
**Curricular Governance:** AICTE Model Postgraduate Curriculum & SPPU Faculty of Management / Science  
**Standard Duration:** 2 Years (4 Semesters)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 4 through Level 6**, with Level 7 Cloud & SRE modules.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Target Outcomes
The Master of Computer Applications curriculum is oriented towards enterprise systems engineering, cloud-native architectures, scalable microservices, full-stack application platforms, and automated software delivery. Graduates are equipped to function as Senior Software Engineers, Cloud Architects, and Technical Leads.
