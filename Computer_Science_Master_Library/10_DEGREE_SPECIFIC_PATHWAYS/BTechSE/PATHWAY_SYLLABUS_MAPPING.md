# B.Tech Software Engineering / CSE Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Technology in Software Engineering (B.Tech SE / CSE)  
**Curricular Governance:** AICTE Model Engineering Curriculum, SPPU Engineering Faculty, NBA / ABET Standards  
**Standard Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 1 through Level 6**, with Level 7 & 8 advanced electives.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Graduate Attributes
The B.Tech in Software Engineering program provides an intensive engineering education covering hardware-software integration, low-level systems programming, operating system kernels, distributed algorithms, compiler architecture, and enterprise cloud systems. Graduates demonstrate the ability to analyze complex computing problems, design fault-tolerant distributed platforms, and optimize hardware-level microarchitectural throughput.
