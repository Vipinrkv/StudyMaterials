# BESE (Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering) Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering (B.E. SE)  
**Curricular Governance:** Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU Engineering Faculty) & AICTE Guidelines  
**Standard Duration:** 4 Years (8 Semesters)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 1 through Level 5**, with Level 6 & Level 7 modules.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Graduate Attributes
The Bachelor of Engineering in Software Engineering curriculum focuses on software architecture, rigorous software quality assurance, formal specifications, system metrics, hardware-software interfacing, and life-cycle verification. Students are trained as professional systems and software engineers capable of designing robust mission-critical systems.
