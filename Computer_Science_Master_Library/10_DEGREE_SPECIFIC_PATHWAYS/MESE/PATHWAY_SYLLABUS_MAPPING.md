# MESE (Master of Engineering in Software Engineering) Academic Pathway & Syllabus Mapping

**Degree Program:** Master of Engineering in Software Engineering (M.E. SE)  
**Curricular Governance:** Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU Engineering Faculty) & AICTE Guidelines  
**Standard Duration:** 2 Years (4 Semesters)  
**Primary Library Levels:** **Level 5 (Professional Software Engineering) through Level 8 (Master Engineer & Researcher)**, with Level 9 research modules.

---

## 1. Degree Profile & Graduate Attributes
The Master of Engineering in Software Engineering curriculum focuses on software metrics, enterprise architecture, automated verification, empirical software engineering, reliability modeling, and software process improvement. Graduates lead complex software initiatives as Chief Systems Architects, VP of Engineering, or Senior Principal Engineers.
