# DEGREE-SPECIFIC CURRICULUM MAPPINGS

This document maps the **9-Level Master Library** directly to the official syllabus requirements of the eight target degrees, referencing Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU / Pune University) CBCS regulations, AICTE model curricula, and international ACM/IEEE-CS guidelines.

---

## 🏛️ Comprehensive Degree Matrix

| Academic Degree | Typical Duration | Semesters | Primary Target Levels | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **BCS** (B.Sc. Computer Science) | 3 or 4 Years | 6–8 Semesters | **Level 1 to Level 4** | Theoretical computer science, mathematics, core programming, databases. |
| **BCA** (Bachelor of Computer Applications) | 3 or 4 Years | 6–8 Semesters | **Level 1 to Level 4, Level 5 (Selected)** | Application development, web tech, database applications, business systems. |
| **MCS** (M.Sc. Computer Science) | 2 Years | 4 Semesters | **Level 4 to Level 6, Level 8 (Selected)** | Advanced theory, algorithms, distributed computing, data science, research. |
| **MCA** (Master of Computer Applications) | 2 Years | 4 Semesters | **Level 4 to Level 6** | Enterprise software engineering, cloud computing, full-stack, DevOps, AI applications. |
| **BESE** (B.E. Software Engineering) | 4 Years | 8 Semesters | **Level 1 to Level 5** | Software architecture, testing, systems engineering, hardware interfaces, SDLC. |
| **MESE** (M.E. Software Engineering) | 2 Years | 4 Semesters | **Level 5 to Level 7** | Quality assurance, formal verification, enterprise architecture, empirical SE. |
| **B.Tech SE / CSE** | 4 Years | 8 Semesters | **Level 1 to Level 5, Level 6 (Electives)** | Rigorous engineering, OS, networking, compilers, cloud, modern software platforms. |
| **M.Tech SE / CSE** | 2 Years | 4 Semesters | **Level 6 to Level 8, Level 9 (Research)** | Distributed systems, advanced AI, systems research, thesis development. |

## 1. BCS (Bachelor of Computer Science) — SPPU CBCS Alignment

- **Year 1 (Sem I & II):**
  - CS-101: Problem Solving Using Computer and 'C' Programming $\rightarrow$ **Level 1 & Level 2**
  - CS-102: Database Management Systems I $\rightarrow$ **Level 2 & Level 3**
  - MTC-101: Discrete Mathematics $\rightarrow$ **Level 2 & Level 3**
  - ELC-101: Electronic Devices and Circuits $\rightarrow$ **Level 1**
- **Year 2 (Sem III & IV):**
  - CS-201: Data Structures and Algorithms $\rightarrow$ **Level 3**
  - CS-202: Software Engineering & Object-Oriented Concepts (C++) $\rightarrow$ **Level 3**
  - CS-203: Relational Database Management Systems (PostgreSQL) $\rightarrow$ **Level 3 & Level 4**
  - CS-204: Computer Networks $\rightarrow$ **Level 3 & Level 4**
- **Year 3 (Sem V & VI):**
  - CS-301: System Programming & Operating Systems (Linux) $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
  - CS-302: Theoretical Computer Science (TCS / Automata) $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
  - CS-303: Compiler Construction $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
  - CS-304: Java Programming & Web Development $\rightarrow$ **Level 3 & Level 5**
  - Electives: Cloud Computing, Machine Learning, Information Security $\rightarrow$ **Level 5 & Level 6**

  - Object-Oriented Programming with C++ / Java $\rightarrow$ **Level 3**
  - Software Engineering $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
- **Year 3 (Sem V & VI):**
  - Python Programming $\rightarrow$ **Level 2 & Level 5**

## 5. BESE & B.Tech Software Engineering — Engineering Alignment

- **Sem I & II (First Year Engineering - F.E.):**
  - Engineering Physics & Chemistry $\rightarrow$ Fundamental Science
  - Engineering Mathematics I & II $\rightarrow$ Foundational Calculus & Linear Algebra
  - Programming and Problem Solving (PPS - Python/C) $\rightarrow$ **Level 1 & Level 2**
  - Basic Electronics Engineering $\rightarrow$ **Level 1**
- **Sem III & IV (Second Year - S.E.):**
  - Discrete Mathematics & Logic $\rightarrow$ **Level 2 & Level 3**
  - Data Structures & Algorithms $\rightarrow$ **Level 3**
  - Digital Electronics & Computer Organization $\rightarrow$ **Level 1 & Level 3**
  - Object-Oriented Programming $\rightarrow$ **Level 3**
  - Microprocessor Architecture $\rightarrow$ **Level 3 & Level 4**
- **Sem V & VI (Third Year - T.E.):**
  - Database Management Systems $\rightarrow$ **Level 3 & Level 4**
  - Theory of Computation $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
  - Operating Systems Internals $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
  - Software Engineering & Agile Process Management $\rightarrow$ **Level 4 & Level 5**
  - Computer Networks & Protocols $\rightarrow$ **Level 4**
- **Sem VII & VIII (Final Year - B.E./B.Tech):**
  - Design & Analysis of Algorithms $\rightarrow$ **Level 4 & Level 6**
  - Cloud Computing & Distributed Systems $\rightarrow$ **Level 6**
  - Machine Learning & AI Engineering $\rightarrow$ **Level 6**
  - High Performance Computing $\rightarrow$ **Level 7 & Level 8**
  - Major Capstone Project (Industry standard) $\rightarrow$ **Level 5 & Level 7**
